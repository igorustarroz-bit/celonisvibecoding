Why you can’t miss Celosphere

The value

Learn to generate value with AI — fast.

Leave with practical strategies for using the Celonis Platform to industrialize Enterprise AI. Hear from and connect with experts to identify use cases and strategies that support your business goals, and learn how to finally get RoAI.

The stories

Hear from changemakers who’ve generated more than $10 billion in value, and learn exactly how they did it.

Pick from curated sessions for a broad range of functions and industries. Get tips, tricks, lessons learned, and best practices from customer speakers who’ve put the Celonis Platform to work across their most business-critical use cases. Past years have seen speakers from BMW, Siemens, Pepsi, Exxon Mobil, Danone, Merck, Reckitt, Lufthansa Group, Pfizer, and more.

The speakers

Mind-blowing talks and panels with world-renowned thinkers and influencers.

You never know who’ll show up at Celosphere. Nobel Prize winners, world leaders, and Fortune 500 CEOs to football stars - to give you a taste. This year you’ll hear from guest speakers on AI, operational excellence, leadership, and what the future holds for AI and process people like you.

The innovation

Be the first to find out about massive leaps for the Celonis Platform.

Celosphere is the launchpad for our biggest Platform innovations. You’ll learn about cutting-edge advances in the Celonis Context Model and across every layer of the platform, plus discover the newest ways to scale and get value from AI, and try out new Platform capabilities.

The people

Make new connections with experts, partners, and peers, and have a little fun while you’re at it.

Join networking meetups throughout the event, book one-on-one time with peers or the Celonis team using our peer-to-peer meeting app, or enjoy mingling during Celosphere’s always-memorable pop-up performances, engaging games, happy hours, and delicious meals.

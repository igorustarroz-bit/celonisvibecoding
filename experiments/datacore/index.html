The Data Core

Data Core enables you to extract data from any source, and query billions of records rapidly. It is the core infrastructure that powers the Celonis Process Intelligence Platform and it is built to scale seamlessly across your business.

Bring in data from wherever it lives

Start flowing data into Celonis fast with 100+ pre-built extractors for on-prem and cloud-based ERPs, CRMs, data warehouses, and more. Stream data in real-time with Apache Kafka or use zero-copy bi-directional integrations to tap into data directly from your lakehouse — and feed Process Intelligence back into it.

Want to capture your offline processes? Our Task Mining capabilities and AI-driven task discovery help you build a richer, more comprehensive digital twin, connecting desktop actions to business processes.

Harmonize data from disparate sources

Celonis Data Core provides a suite of integrated tools for data integration and harmonization, making it easy to access, refine, and orchestrate data transformations across diverse sources.

Get complete data visibility and traceability at every step – whether it’s cleansing, deduplication, or harmonization. Data Core helps you manage it all in a unified experience.

Query data at unparalleled speed and scale

Celonis Data Core is powered by the most powerful process query engine on the market. It handles up to 20x more data than the competition and delivers the fastest and most consistent end-to-end load times. The use-cases you can go after are limited only by your creativity.
